About Dan

Designer and front-end developer in Poole, Dorset.

In short

I’m Dan Laws. I co-founded Sandbanks Digital, a two-person studio in Poole that designs and builds websites, web apps and online shops for UK small businesses, with everything made in-house.

I lead design and front-end build. In practice that means taking a business from a first conversation to a site that is fast, clear and easy for them to run, and building the products we sell ourselves along the way.

I have a First Class degree in Computer Science and a master’s in Cyber Security, both from the University of the West of England. Alongside the studio I work on a university service desk, where much of the job is spotting and shutting down account compromise.

Before all of that came years of retail, mostly at B&Q, where I learned to explain technical things plainly and to see a customer’s order through to the end.
