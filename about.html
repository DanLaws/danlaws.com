About Dan

Designer and front-end developer in Poole, Dorset.

In short

I’m Dan Laws. I co-founded Sandbanks Digital, a two-person studio in Poole that designs and builds websites, web apps and online shops for UK small businesses, with everything made in-house.

I lead design and front-end build. In practice that means taking a business from a first conversation to a site that is fast, clear and easy for them to run, and building the products we sell ourselves along the way.

Before that, and alongside it, I’ve spent five-plus years working and studying in tech.

What I do

Design

Layout, type and the words on the page, worked out with the business owner before anything is built.

Front end

Fast, accessible pages, from plain HTML on a small server up to full Next.js and React apps.

Products

Taking an idea from a first sketch to something people pay for, then keeping it running.

Tools I reach for

Front end

Next.js, React, Vite and Tailwind for apps. Plain HTML, CSS and a little JavaScript for sites that don’t need more, like this one.

Back end and data

Node, FastAPI and Flask, on Postgres or Supabase, with Redis and Celery when there’s background work to do.

Shipping

Railway, Vercel and Cloudflare for hosting; Stripe for payments; Shopify when a shop is the right answer.
