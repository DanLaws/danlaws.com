What I hold to

Build it properly, in-house. Everything we make at Sandbanks is designed and coded by the two of us, so the person you talk to is the person who builds it.

Fast and plain beats clever. Static pages where they will do, a framework where it earns its place, and nothing a visitor has to sit and wait for.

A launch is the start, not the end. Sites and products get looked after, measured and improved for as long as they are running.

Small businesses deserve good tools. Most of what I build is for owners who run everything themselves, so it has to be simple to use and honest about what it costs.
